Required cases actually execute; errors/skips/xfails do not count as passes. Map results to requirement IDs and assert negative controls fail when violated. Resolve type/dependency/contract/security/permission checks from actual merged team tooling and record commands/versions/exits; do not invent unavailable commands or declare all static gates passed from lint.
